## What is this?

Smoke from wildfires can make asthma worse. We made a tool called AirPlan. Each
evening, it looks at the smoke forecast for tomorrow. Then it sends your family
a plan: green, yellow, or red.

We want to see if AirPlan can learn how smoke affects **you**.

## What would happen?

- Every evening this summer and fall, you or your parent answers 6 quick
  questions. You just tap. It takes about 10 seconds.
- The questions ask how your breathing was today, if you used your rescue
  inhaler, if you woke up at night, how long you were outside, if you had a
  cold, and if you took your daily medicine.
- Your family gets a green, yellow, or red plan for the next day.

It is okay to miss a day.

## Important

- AirPlan is **not** your doctor. Keep following the asthma plan from your doctor.
- AirPlan can be wrong.
- If it is very hard to breathe or talk, tell an adult right away. They should
  call 911.
