## Why we are doing this study

Wildfire smoke can make asthma worse. Some children react to smoke more than
others. AirPlan is a tool that takes the next day's smoke forecast and sends a
green, yellow, or red plan for one child. It tries to learn how your child
reacts to smoke from short daily check-ins.

So far AirPlan has only been tested on made-up (simulated) children. This study
asks whether it works any better than the same plan for everyone when it learns
from real families.

## What AirPlan is NOT

- It is **not medical advice** and does not diagnose or treat asthma.
- It **never replaces** the asthma action plan from your child's doctor. Keep
  following that plan and your child's medicines exactly as prescribed.
- Forecasts can be wrong. If your child has trouble breathing or talking, has
  blue or gray lips or nails, or the rescue inhaler is not helping, **call 911**.

## What you would do

- **Once:** tell us your ZIP code, your child's age group (6-11 or 12-17), and
  whether your child has a daily controller medicine.
- **Each evening, June 1 to October 31, 2027:** answer 6 quick questions by
  tapping (about 10 seconds). They ask about today's asthma symptoms, rescue
  inhaler puffs, waking at night, time outdoors, colds, and the controller medicine.
- **Each evening:** get tomorrow's plan by text or WhatsApp.
- **At the end (optional):** a 5-question survey about how clear and easy it was.

Missing a day is fine. You never have to explain.

## What we collect, and what we do not

We collect your check-in answers, your ZIP code, your child's age group, and
the plans we sent. All of it is stored under a random code (like P07), not a name.

We do **not** collect your names in the study data, your street address, your
child's birth date, medical records, your doctor's name, or your child's school.

Your phone number is kept in a separate locked file, only so we can send plans.

## Possible risks

- A forecast could be wrong, so a plan could say green on a bad day or red on a
  fine day. Always use your own judgment and your doctor's plan.
- Answering every day takes a little time.
- There is a small chance of a privacy problem. The steps above are there to
  keep that chance low.

## Possible benefits

Your child may or may not benefit. Your help may show whether personal smoke
plans are worth building for other families.
