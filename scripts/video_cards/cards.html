How Serein works

What exists today

NOAA's smoke forecast, live readings from about 400 EPA monitors in the West, weather and fire detections. All free public data.

→

Serein

A graph neural network treats the monitors as a network: each one learns from its neighbors and corrects NOAA's forecast.

→

For a family

A forecast for their neighborhood, one to three days ahead, with a range, and a green, yellow or red plan for their child.

checked against NOAA's and EPA's own forecasts

Families never pay.

Organizations that act on smoke days pay a yearly subscription.

School districts

$500–$1,000

per school building a year, a quarter to half of one emergency-software license

Health plans and children's hospitals

$10–$20

per enrolled child with asthma a year

Air districts and health departments

License

to put the forecast in the alerts they already send

Inputs: free public data from NOAA, NASA and EPA. The model runs in a fraction of a second on an ordinary computer.
