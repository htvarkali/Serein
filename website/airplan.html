AirPlan

What could a smoke forecast look like for one family?

AirPlan is a research prototype for making wildfire smoke forecasts easier for families of children with asthma to read and discuss.

How it works

What could a check-in add?

AirPlan combines a smoke forecast with a simulated symptom history. The model starts with an estimate for a group and updates its estimate for a fictional child as new examples are added.

Ozone and temperature can provide additional context when their forecasts cover the full day. The research team still needs to test whether the model can predict symptoms for real children.

Proposed research pilot

What would a family study test?

A future school or clinic study could evaluate whether families find the forecasts clear and can keep up with short check-ins. Clinical and research review come first.
